A change in the operating model

From fragments to a defensible account.

The fragmented handoff
  • A trace link without downstream state.
  • A tool response treated as a confirmed write.
  • A scope estimate without a named owner.
  • A proposal without an explicit decision boundary.
The accountable handoff
  • Original identifiers carried through the incident.
  • Observed evidence distinguished from inference.
  • Affected records with ownership context.
  • A deliberate review and recorded rationale.
