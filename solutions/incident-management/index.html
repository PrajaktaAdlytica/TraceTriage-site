The views that matter

Start with the question you own.

Incident Review

Move from a tool response to the records it may have affected. Separate observed changes from inference, then put an owner beside the scope.

↗

Incident Proof

Bring the scope, evidence and intended response to the person who can authorize it. A containment proposal is the beginning of a decision, not its completion.

A useful evaluation

Agree the question.
Then inspect the answer.

  • Can the initiating write be reconstructed?
  • Can each affected record be traced to evidence?
  • Is ownership resolved or explicitly unknown?
  • Can the human decision be reviewed later?
