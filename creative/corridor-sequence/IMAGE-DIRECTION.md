# Image generation direction

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using the attached corridor as the scene reference. Each successive frame uses the preceding generated view as a reference, preserving the material palette, principal mountain, sunset direction and evergreen setting.

## Shared constraints

One complete photorealistic 16:9 landscape image per frame. Bronze vertical ribs and pale warm stone columns on the left; black perforated server cabinets with tiny amber lights on the right; polished dark stone floor; tall end window; gray-blue evergreen forest and valley mist; snow-dusted mountain peak with the sun to its right. Natural photographic materials, clean optics, detailed foliage and mist. No lettering, branding, people, UI, collage, added furnishings, opaque smoke wall or giant flare.

## 01 / Wide corridor

Preserve the reference's original establishing view and eye-height perspective down the aisle. Keep the end window realistically distant. Preserve the existing narrow amber floor strip and square floor recess. Improve fine material detail without adding objects or changing the environment.

## 02 / Forward through the corridor

Move the camera several meters closer to the same end window. The window becomes appreciably taller and wider; foreground wall ribs and cabinets pass out of frame through parallax. The floor recess moves toward the lower edge. Same mountain profile, sun direction and forest identity. Slightly shifted layered mist between trees.

## 03 / Window approach

Move to immediately in front of the window. The window dominates the frame; only slender corridor edges and a narrow floor strip remain. Keep the thin central vertical mullion and the original mountains/trees. The recess and amber floor strip have passed beneath the camera. Focus increasingly on the clear view beyond the glass and visibly layered outdoor mist.

## 04 / Forest and mountain focus

Move close to the glass and reframe slightly into the clear pane. The central mullion moves toward the extreme right edge. Corridor surfaces and sill leave the crop; the view is still from inside through the glass. Preserve the principal mountain, sun and forest arrangement. Sharply resolve evergreen foliage and separate cool mist bands with translucent curls and open gaps. This is the ending composition for a near-still camera hold while Flow animates the fog.

The keyframes are visual references rather than a calibrated 3D reconstruction. Inspect generated transitions for straight architecture and scenery consistency before using the finished film. Exact pixel dimensions are recorded in `manifest.json` without an artificial upscale.
