Do not change a form to say “sent” until its delivery endpoint confirms success. Do not change the access page to say a link was emailed without identity-service confirmation. Production responses require independently verified source permissions and human authority; the demonstration does not provide that execution layer.

After deployment, repeat direct-route, asset, responsive, reduced-motion, menu, modal and form checks against the production origin. Confirm social-image URLs and canonical tags resolve to the connected domain.
